Welcome to FarelMC — Farel's cozy survival hangout for friends! A few simple things so everyone has a good time:

• Be kind — no griefing, stealing, or messing with other people's builds.
• No cheats or hacked clients — play fair, that's the fun of it.
• Keep chat friendly — no spam, no harassment.
• When in doubt, just ask! Use /tpa to visit friends instead of wandering into their base uninvited.

Need a hand? Try /help for all the commands, or ask Farel. Have fun and happy crafting!
