Welcome to FarelMC! This is Farel's cozy survival hangout for friends. A few simple things so everyone has a good time:

* Be kind. No griefing, stealing, or messing with other people's builds.
* No cheats or hacked clients. Play fair, that is the fun of it.
* Keep chat friendly. No spam, no harassment.
* Respect claims and bases. When in doubt, just ask!
* Use /tpa to visit friends instead of wandering into their base uninvited.

Need a hand? Try /help for all the commands, or ask Farel. Bedrock and phone players use /register then /login each session. Java players log in automatically. Have fun and happy crafting!
